# Privacy Policy — English Grade 11 Study

Last updated: November 18, 2026

## 1. Information We Collect
When you choose **Sign in with Google**, **English Grade 11 Study** requests basic Google identity profile information (`openid`, `email`, and `profile`: your email address, display name, and profile picture) strictly to verify student and teacher identity and keep each student profile linked to one Google account across devices.

If you explicitly enable optional Google Drive backup (`drive.appdata` and `drive.file`), the application stores only your own study backup file inside your personal Google Drive.

## 2. How We Use Information
- Verify student and teacher identities during sign-in.
- Lock each student profile to its linked Google account to prevent unauthorized cross-device linking.
- Sync earned points, stars, lessons, and homework completion with your class teacher.

## 3. Data Sharing
We do **not** sell, rent, trade, or share student or teacher personal data with third parties or advertisers.

## 4. Account Unlinking & Data Removal
Students or parents can contact the teacher at any time to unlink a Google account or remove student data from class records.
